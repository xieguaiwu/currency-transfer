FX Pixel is a privacy-friendly app for global currency
conversion and historical inflation comparison.

Exchange tab
- Live rates for 160+ currencies (open.er-api.com)
- Convert any amount between two currencies
- See both directions of the rate
- Refresh on demand

Inflation tab
- Compare purchasing power between two years
- World Bank CPI data (consumer price index)
- Cumulative inflation and average annual rate
- Buying-power equivalent (e.g. 100 USD in 2015)
- 1990 to current year

Search
- Find any currency by code or name

Privacy
- No ads, no tracking, no analytics
- No account required
- Only INTERNET permission
- All traffic is HTTPS
- No data stored locally

Data sources
- open.er-api.com (free, no key)
- World Bank Indicators API (free, no key)

Note: the app depends on public network services for
live data. If those services are unavailable, the app
shows an error and offers retry.
